**What changed.** The island now has a way onto it and a way up it.

- *The path.* Found, not drawn (`shared/path.ts`, `world.path`): the cheapest walk from a
  landing on the near shore to the summit, by a search over the land that charges for climbing
  steeper than about one in three and for turning, so it takes the steep face in slanting legs.
  The terrain wears it into the grass from a small distance-field texture (`terrain/wear.ts`):
  bare earth down the middle, yellowed grass beside it, and the same clean edge on the phone's
  coarse ground as on the desktop's. One new colour, `path`.
- *Waymarks.* Five small cairns beside the path, each on the side clear of its other legs: the
  summit cairn in small.
- *The landing.* Two rows of stones from the top of the beach into the water, the sand between
  them cleared for a keel, and a stone stood on end at the head of each row.
- *Keeping off.* Boulders and tufts no longer lie on the path, nor boulders in the cairn. They
  are left out after their random draws are taken, so every other one is where it was.
- *The tour walks.* A leg can now be walked instead of flown (`rig/walk.ts`). From the new
  `landing` view the camera comes in over the water between the rows, goes up the path at eye
  height looking where it is going, slows for the bends, and stops below the cairn, black
  against the sun (`climb`). Then it pulls back out to sea until the whole island is in the
  frame (`island`).
- *Four postcards:* `landing`, `climb`, `island` and `east`.

In `hero` the facing slope was grass and boulders. It now carries a pale zigzag from the beach
to the shoulder under the cairn, five waymarks up it, and the landing's stones running into the
water at lower left. `hero` has not moved, and no boulder or tuft in it has moved.

**Something broken, found on the way.** The tour's last leg, `ridge` to `hero`, joined two
views that face opposite ways and swung the camera right round as it crossed the island: 72
degrees a second at worst on `main`. The `east` postcard now stands between them, and the
fastest turn anywhere on the loop is 29. A test walks the whole loop and fails over 35 degrees
or 35 units a second.

**Why this, today.** No open requests and a clean ledger, so the top chapter of the roadmap,
taken whole: landing, path, waymarks, the tour following it, and the postcard from above.

**What it cost.** From the gate, hero view: draw calls 10 → 12 on both tiers (budgets 160 and
100), which is the waymarks' one mesh and its shadow pass. Triangles 132,102 → 138,153 on
desktop and 66,206 → 73,192 on mobile (800,000 and 300,000). Live textures 9 → 10 (the path's,
64 KB at most on desktop); programs still 8. No leak, same picture under two other clocks. The
path is found once at start, about 45 ms here together with the summit search it shares.
`diff` against `main`: hero 3.5% of pixels (a path is a thin thing; the worst block of the
frame is 80% changed), summit 2.7%. The loop grew from 100 s to 207 s: four more views, and 42
seconds on foot.

**Worth knowing.**
- `world.path.at(distance)` gives a place on the path and which way it runs there;
  `distanceTo(x, z)` says how far a place is from it. Build beside the path with these.
- A view with `walk: true` is left on foot. `rig/route.ts` builds the world's tour, for the rig
  and for the tests alike.
- To drop something from a scatter without moving the rest: take its draws, then skip it.
- `landing` and `climb` are typed in at the path's foot and 68 units up it. If the path moves
  they must follow; the tour test says when the walk has left it.
- The walk's pace comes from how fast its gaze would turn (`HEAD_TURN` in `walk.ts`).
- The lower path lies under a cloud's shadow at second ten. That is the weather, not the path.
