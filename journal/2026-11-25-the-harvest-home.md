The roadmap's third chapter, taken before `Inside` and the night because it is the one that
shows at second ten: the owner asked for days that can be seen in the thumbnail. Built
whole, and grown by what the season brings with a harvest.

**The kiln.** `shared/yard.ts` puts it on the level shelf between the fold and the field
gate, the one lit green the walls had left empty, in the middle of `hero`. A round tower of
the house's stone, each ring a little smaller than the one under it, its back let into the
hill and its fire-hole on the low side under one long stone; a cone of turf over it, and a
thin smoke out of the top that leans with the house's (`shared/smoke.ts`, moved out of the
shelter, takes a smaller `fire`). A way is worn to its mouth from the path.

**The stacks.** Four on the green before the door, the levellest ground the island has: a
beehive of sheaves on a ring of stones, thatched, eight ropes over it and one round, a stone
hung on every rope's end. The last is half built, the sledge beside it with three sheaves
still on, and the ladder is left on the one thatched before it. There is room for four and
no wall: a walled yard did not fit between the path and the house.

**The field.** The barley's first two rigs are bare stubble, carried; two stand in stooks;
the last still bows to the wind.

**The year.** With the harvest the birches turn: a lump of leaves is the barley's gold by
where it hangs and how far its tree leans, so the wood yellows from its edge in and the
lone birches are nearly all gold. No new colour.

**In the picture.** Before and after `hero` side by side at thumbnail size: the right third
is flecked gold where it was green, a round building smokes in the middle, gold domes stand
left of the house, the field is pale. `diff` moves 19 of 22 views; `climb`, `shore` and
`mill` are as they were, and the sky, the sea and the flock in `hero` did not move a pixel.
The new postcard `yard` looks in over the field's dyke: mill, cairn, kiln and its smoke,
the path going up, the stacks and the ladder, the lamp in the door. Looked at on both tiers.

**The day.** `yard` needed eight seconds and the afternoon had none: the roadmap said the
next view wanted the day's pace retuned first. Done at the root: the slow part of the day is
now typed in as its length and how far the sky turns in it (`NIGHT`, `TURNED`), and second
ten as an hour (`TEN`), so `DAY` grew from 313.5 to 321.5 s and every stop from `hero` to
`ridge` kept its sun to a tenth of a degree (`ridge` 0.52 up, as before; second ten
9.739, as before). The next afternoon stop only makes `DAY` longer.

**Fitting it in.** Grass, tufts, heather, boulders, trods and sheep keep off the yard and
its ways; tests hold it off the path, the house, the walls, the trees, every camera and the
tour. Its three materials are made like the house's, so no program was added. The mill's
board helper became `shared/boards.ts`, the sheaf `shared/sheaf.ts`.

**Cost, from the gate.** Desktop 82 draws and 616,570 triangles before; 91 and 635,508
after (79% of 800,000). Mobile 70 and 238,223 before; 75 and 234,717 after (78% of 300,000):
a phone was at 79% and the yard took it to 82%, so room was made there, more than was used.
On a phone the dykes' cope and the house's stones throw no shadow of their own (the wall
under the one and the dark block inside the other do), and the stacks have no ropes.
Programs 29 of 40, as before; textures 10. Desktop is a hair under the 80% where the next
day must make room before it builds.
