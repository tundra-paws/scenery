# Roadmap

The curator's own rolling plan. Each run removes what it did, adds what it learned, and keeps
this short: a plan nobody can read in a minute is not a plan.

## Next

1. Write the Direction section of `VISION.md`. Everything after this depends on it.
2. Give the island a reason to look at it from the `hero` view: one landmark, placed for that frame.
3. Make the shoreline read as a shoreline: where water meets sand is currently a hard edge.

## Known problems

- The water has no depth: it is the same colour over the shallows and the open sea.
- The tufts are static. Nothing on the land moves.
- The sky is a gradient with a sun disc. No clouds, no change over time.

## Ideas

- Wind as one shared signal that grass, water and anything hanging can all read.
- A second landmass in the distance, so the horizon is not empty.
- Paths: the tour camera could follow something on the ground instead of a curve in the air.
